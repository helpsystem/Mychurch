== Description ==

= Aras Tax Services - Professional Tax & Accounting Plugin =

A complete WordPress plugin solution for **Aras Tax Services** featuring branded components, interactive tax calculators, service showcases, and contact management.

= Features =

* **🎯 Tax Calculator** - Interactive federal income tax & self-employment tax calculator
* **📋 Service Showcase** - Beautiful service cards for Strategy, Financial Consulting, Business Planning
* **📄 Tax Forms Download** - W-4, 1040, 1099-NEC form downloads
* **📞 Contact Widget** - Sidebar widget with phone, email, and address
* **🎨 Branded Design** - Navy blue & gold color scheme matching Aras Tax branding
* **⚙️ Admin Dashboard** - Full settings page to customize contact info, colors, and content
* **📱 Responsive** - Mobile-friendly design for all components

= Available Shortcodes =

* `[aras_tax_banner]` - Branded banner with header text and CTA buttons
* `[aras_tax_calculator]` - Interactive tax calculator
* `[aras_tax_contact]` - Contact information section
* `[aras_tax_w4_download]` - W-4 form download card
* `[aras_tax_services]` - Services showcase grid
* `[aras_tax_team]` - Team members display
* `[aras_tax_1040_download]` - 1040 form download card
* `[aras_tax_1099_download]` - 1099-NEC form download card

= Admin Settings =

Navigate to **Aras Tax** in your WordPress admin dashboard to configure:
- Phone number, email, and address
- Header text and subtext
- Primary and accent colors
- Banner visibility toggle
- Tax year configuration

= Why Choose Aras Tax Services Plugin? =

This plugin is specifically designed for **Aras Tax Services** (aras-cpa.com) with professional branding and a focus on providing valuable tax tools to your website visitors.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the `aras-tax-plugin` folder to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu in WordPress
3. Navigate to **Aras Tax** in the admin menu to configure settings
4. Use shortcodes to add components to your pages/posts

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= How do I change the tax calculator to use my own rates? =

Edit the `$tax_brackets` and `$standard_deductions` arrays in `includes/class-aras-tax-tax-calculator.php`

= Can I customize the colors? =

Yes! Go to **Aras Tax > Dashboard** in your WordPress admin to change primary and accent colors.

= How do I add the contact widget to my sidebar? =

Go to **Appearance > Widgets**, find the **Aras Tax Services Widget**, and drag it to your desired sidebar.
